# Apex v1.5

Further modularization and hardware profiles.

## What's new

- **Hardware profiles** — New `Modules/Profiles.ps1`. Detects LowRAM, Hybrid, Laptop, DiscreteGPU, HighCore. Press `p` to view the profile and optionally apply the recommended Advanced preset.
- Modular structure continues. Hardware detection, adaptive tweaks, Expert mode, and metrics remain.
- Safe / Advanced / Extreme presets, restore points, dry-run, and selective revert unchanged.

## Download

- Download `Apex.bat`, `Apex.ps1`, and the `Modules` folder.
- Right-click `Apex.bat` → Run as administrator.
- `s` Safe, `ad` Advanced, `e` Extreme, `x` Expert, `p` Profile, then `a` to apply.

## Safety

Nothing changes until you apply. Restore point is attempted. High-risk items always ask for confirmation.
