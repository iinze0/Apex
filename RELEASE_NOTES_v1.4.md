# Apex v1.4

Expert mode UI and expanded metrics.

## What's new

- **Expert mode** — Press `x` in the menu to open Expert mode. High-risk options are highlighted in red and listed separately.
- **Expanded metrics** — `Apex-metrics.txt` now also records uptime (hours) and a simple responsiveness sample (ms) before and after apply.
- Modular structure, hardware detection, and adaptive tweaks remain.
- Safe / Advanced / Extreme presets, restore points, dry-run, and selective revert unchanged.

## Download

- Download `Apex.bat`, `Apex.ps1`, and the `Modules` folder.
- Right-click `Apex.bat` → Run as administrator.
- `s` Safe, `ad` Advanced, `e` Extreme, `x` Expert, then `a` to apply.

## Safety

Nothing changes until you apply. Restore point is attempted. High-risk items always ask for confirmation.
